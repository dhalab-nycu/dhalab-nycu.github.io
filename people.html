01 / PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Kai-Chun Liu, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor

Department of Biomedical Engineering
National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University

Dr. Liu's research focuses on wearable sensing, digital biomarkers, human movement analysis, and machine learning for health assessment. His work aims to translate multimodal sensor data into clinically meaningful measures of health and physical function in everyday environments.
